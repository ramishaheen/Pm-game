# 03 — Narrative & Mission Breakdown

The campaign is structured in **five Acts** and ~18 missions. Most are
non-combat; the three battle missions are explicitly flagged. Each mission
lists its **perspective** (1P-Prophet = first person as the Prophet, no avatar;
1P-Companion = first person as a named companion, used to *witness* the Prophet
and feel persecution from inside), its **core verbs**, and its **emotional
target**.

A guiding rule: *the mission objective is almost never "defeat." It is "hold to
principle."*

---

## ACT I — The Trustworthy (Mecca, before & at revelation)

### M1 · "Al-Amīn" — Rebuilding the Kaʿba  ★ prototype
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet.
- **Verbs:** observe, listen, judge, resolve.
- **Beat:** The clans are at the edge of bloodshed over who places the Black
  Stone. The player gathers testimony, then chooses *how* to resolve it. The
  honored solution — the cloak lifted by all clans together — is discovered,
  not handed to you, so the player *earns* the insight that shared honor defuses
  pride.
- **Emotional target:** the quiet authority of fairness; conflict dissolved
  without a single blow.

### M2 · "Iqraʾ" — The Cave of Ḥirāʾ
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet.
- **Verbs:** retreat, reflect, endure the overwhelming.
- **Beat:** Solitude on the mountain; the world falls away. Revelation is
  rendered as overwhelming **light and sound and pressure** (R5) — never a
  creature. The player returns home shaken; Khadīja's belief and comfort is the
  emotional anchor that closes the mission.
- **Emotional target:** awe, fear, and being *held* by a loved one.

## ACT II — The Weight of Truth (persecution)

### M3 · "The First Circle" — the private invitation
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet.
- **Verbs:** confide, persuade, build trust.
- **Beat:** Quietly sharing the message with those closest. Branching dialogue
  with Khadīja, ʿAlī, Zayd, Abū Bakr — each won by sincerity, not pressure.

### M4 · "Aḥad, Aḥad" — Bilāl under the rock
- **Perspective:** 1P-Companion (Bilāl), then Abū Bakr.
- **Verbs:** endure, refuse, then ransom/free.
- **Beat:** The player *feels* persecution from the inside — heat, weight,
  the demand to recant — and holds. Resolution is **Abū Bakr buying his
  freedom**: liberation, not vengeance. This is the game's thesis mission about
  dignity.
- **Emotional target:** unbearable pressure met with unbreakable conviction;
  then relief and human solidarity.

### M5 · "The Valley" — the boycott
- **Perspective:** 1P-Companion (a youth of Banū Hāshim).
- **Verbs:** survive, share, sustain morale.
- **Beat:** Three years of scarcity compressed into a tense resource-and-care
  vignette: distributing what little food there is, protecting the elders and
  children, keeping hope. No enemy to shoot — the antagonist is hardship
  itself.

### M6 · "Refuge Across the Sea" — Abyssinia
- **Perspective:** 1P-Companion (Jaʿfar ibn Abī Ṭālib).
- **Verbs:** travel, seek asylum, testify.
- **Beat:** Before the Najāshī's court, the player chooses how to answer the
  Quraysh envoys — the historical path is honest testimony (reciting the verses
  on Jesus and Mary), winning protection. A mission about **refuge,
  interfaith respect, and the power of honest speech.**

## ACT III — Sorrow & the Turn Outward

### M7 · "The Year of Sorrow"
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet.
- **Verbs:** grieve, sit vigil, carry on.
- **Beat:** The deaths of Abū Ṭālib and Khadīja. Deliberately low on mechanics
  — presence, memory, the empty places in the home. The player is *allowed to
  feel loss* without a task bar.
- **Emotional target:** grief, and the resolve to continue despite it.

### M8 · "Ṭāʾif" — rejection and the choice of mercy
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet.
- **Verbs:** appeal, withstand, **choose mercy**.
- **Beat:** Rejected and stoned, bloodied, the player is offered (narratively)
  the power to see the town destroyed. The defining choice: **pray for their
  descendants instead.** The game makes mercy the harder, deliberate input —
  and the only way forward.
- **Emotional target:** the radical strength of forgiving those who hurt you.

### M9 · "The Pledges" — al-ʿAqaba
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet.
- **Verbs:** meet in secret, build alliance, earn trust.
- **Beat:** Night meetings with the people of Yathrib; quiet diplomacy that
  opens the path to the Hijra.

## ACT IV — The Migration & a New Community

### M10 · "The Bed" — ʿAlī's sacrifice (decoy)
- **Perspective:** 1P-Companion (ʿAlī).
- **Verbs:** risk yourself for another, hold nerve.
- **Beat:** Lying in the Prophet's bed while assassins gather outside. Tension
  through stillness and trust.

### M11 · "The Cave of Thawr"
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet (with Abū Bakr beside you).
- **Verbs:** hide, trust, reassure.
- **Beat:** Searchers stand at the cave mouth; voices and footsteps just
  outside. Abū Bakr's fear, and the Prophet's calm: *"Do not grieve; God is
  with us."* A stealth/hold sequence whose real subject is **trust in God under
  mortal fear.**

### M12 · "Madīna" — building the community
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet.
- **Verbs:** build, pair, draft a covenant.
- **Beat:** Raising the mosque; the **brotherhood** between Muhājirūn and
  Anṣār; agreeing the **Constitution of Medina** with the city's tribes,
  Jewish tribes included. A constructive, hopeful mission — the relief after
  exile.

## ACT V — Trials, Return, and Farewell

### M13 · "Badr" — the first stand  ⚔️ combat
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet (command/witness) + playable companion melee.
- **Verbs:** position, defend, prevail against the odds.
- **Beat:** Era-accurate defensive battle. Framed as survival of the community,
  not conquest; the aftermath emphasizes the **treatment of captives** (ransom,
  teaching literacy in exchange for freedom).

### M14 · "Uḥud" — the cost of breaking ranks  ⚔️ combat
- **Perspective:** 1P-Companion (an archer on the hill).
- **Verbs:** hold the line — and feel the consequence of leaving it.
- **Beat:** The player *is* one of the archers ordered to hold the ridge. The
  design lets the temptation to chase spoils be real; abandoning the post turns
  the battle and leads to the martyrdom of **Ḥamza**. A mission about
  **discipline, obedience to a plan, and living with consequences** — defeat
  that teaches.

### M15 · "The Trench" — al-Khandaq  ⚔️ siege/defense
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet + companion labor.
- **Verbs:** consult, dig, endure the siege.
- **Beat:** **Salmān's** idea — a trench — turns numbers into irrelevance.
  Emphasis on **consultation (shūrā)** and collective effort over individual
  heroics.

### M16 · "Ḥudaybiyya" — the patient treaty
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet.
- **Verbs:** negotiate, accept hard terms, take the long view.
- **Beat:** Signing a treaty that looks like a loss and proves a victory. The
  hardest "win" in the game to accept emotionally — restraint as strategy.

### M17 · "Fatḥ Makka" — the return and the amnesty  ★ climax
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet.
- **Verbs:** enter, restrain, **forgive**.
- **Beat:** Returning in strength to the city that exiled and tortured the
  community — with the power to take revenge — and instead declaring **general
  amnesty**: *"Go, for you are free."* Idols cleared from the Kaʿba. The entire
  game's moral arc lands here: **mercy at the summit of power.**
- **Emotional target:** catharsis; the realization that the whole journey was
  building to forgiveness, not triumph.

### M18 · "Farewell" — ʿArafa and after
- **Perspective:** 1P-Prophet.
- **Verbs:** address, bless, let go.
- **Beat:** The Farewell Sermon — equality, sanctity of life, the end of
  blood-feuds — delivered to a vast plain of pilgrims (handled with reverence,
  his words as narration). A quiet epilogue on his passing and the community's
  grief and continuity.
- **Emotional target:** completion, legacy, and the passing of a light that
  endures.

---

## Structural notes

- **Perspective rhythm.** Alternating 1P-Prophet (conviction, leadership,
  choice) and 1P-Companion (persecution, sacrifice, witnessing him) keeps the
  red line intact *and* widens the emotional range.
- **Choice without "fail-by-violence."** Most missions cannot be failed by
  dying in combat; they're failed by abandoning principle (and usually offer a
  reflective retry rather than a harsh game-over).
- **Hub between acts.** Quiet "home" interludes (Mecca house, then Medina)
  let the player breathe, revisit characters, and read optional historical
  codex entries.
