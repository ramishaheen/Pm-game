# 05 — Psychological & Emotional Design

You asked for the game to "affect players psychologically." This document is
the engineering of that — the deliberate, ethical techniques used to make the
experience emotionally and morally affecting, and the guardrails that keep it
from becoming manipulative or distressing in harmful ways.

## The intended transformation

The player should leave the game having *felt*, not just learned:

- what it costs to tell the truth when truth is dangerous;
- the suffocating weight of persecution — and the dignity of enduring it;
- grief, and the discipline to keep going through it;
- and above all, the almost unbearable strength it takes to **forgive when you
  hold all the power.**

The whole campaign is a slow climb toward M17 (Fatḥ Makka), so that the moment
of amnesty lands as catharsis — the player realizes the journey was never about
winning; it was about becoming someone who can forgive.

## Techniques (and how each is used)

### 1. Presence over exposition
First person + diegetic narration means the player *stands inside* events. We
show the rock on Bilāl's chest from his own eyes rather than narrating it.
Embodiment is the strongest empathy tool we have.

### 2. Constraint and helplessness, used carefully
In persecution missions the player is *deliberately given limited power* — you
cannot simply fight your way out. The discomfort is the point: it builds
empathy for the historically powerless. **Guardrail:** these sequences are
bounded, never gratuitous, and always resolve into solidarity or relief (e.g.,
Bilāl freed) so the player is not left in despair.

### 3. The "restraint mechanic"
At Ṭāʾif and Fatḥ Makka, the interface offers the *capacity* for revenge and
makes **not using it** the deliberate, effortful input — a held button, a
turning-away, a lowering of the hand. Choosing mercy should feel like an
*action*, costly and intentional, not a passive cutscene. This is the game's
signature emotional mechanic.

### 4. Silence and stillness as design
Some of the most affecting missions (Year of Sorrow, the empty home) strip away
objectives and HUD entirely. Giving the player *nothing to do but feel* is a
deliberate, powerful choice. Music drops out; ambient sound and light carry the
weight.

### 5. Consequence and memory
Uḥud lets the player make the historical mistake (leaving the post) and live
its cost. Earlier choices and losses are remembered and referenced later, so
the world feels like it holds the player's history.

### 6. Earned catharsis
The emotional payoff of M17 is *withheld and built toward* across ~16 missions
of hardship, so forgiveness at the summit is overwhelming rather than cheap.
Pacing is the delivery system for catharsis.

### 7. Sensory craft
Light (the recurring motif of *nūr*), spatial audio, nasheed/era-appropriate
music, weather, and crowd presence are tuned to the emotional target of each
mission. See `07-art-direction.md`.

## Ethical guardrails (mandatory)

Psychological impact must never tip into harm or manipulation:

- **No despair without resolution.** Heavy sequences resolve into dignity,
  solidarity, or hope. We do not leave the player in trauma for effect.
- **No torture-as-spectacle.** Persecution is conveyed with restraint and
  implication, not graphic gore. (Suitable for a wide, including younger,
  audience.)
- **No coercive dark patterns.** No artificial guilt loops, no manipulative
  monetization, no exploiting religious devotion for engagement metrics.
  Sacred acts are never gamified (R6).
- **Content guidance & comfort options.** Up-front content notes; the ability
  to slow or skip the most intense moments; no jump-scares on sacred content.
- **Respect over shock.** When reverence and intensity conflict, reverence
  wins. The goal is to move the player toward empathy and reflection, never to
  distress them gratuitously.

## Measuring success

Not by playtime or "engagement," but by whether playtesters report **empathy,
reflection, and a changed understanding** — and by scholarly and community
reviewers affirming the work is respectful and true.
