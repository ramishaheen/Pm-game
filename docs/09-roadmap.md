# 09 — Roadmap

A phased, realistic plan. A game at the requested fidelity is a multi-year,
multi-disciplinary effort; this sequences it so value and trust are proven
early.

## Phase 0 — Foundation (this repository) ✅
- Vision, principles, and the respectful-design red lines.
- Seerah research compiled into a mission-ready reference.
- Full narrative & mission breakdown (5 acts, ~18 missions).
- First-person-without-depiction design solved.
- Psychological-design framework + ethical guardrails.
- Runnable browser prototype of the core conscience loop (M1).

## Phase 1 — Scholarly & community validation
- Convene qualified scholarly reviewers across major schools of thought.
- Validate the depiction approach, the perspective design, and each mission's
  historical content. Adjust before any production spend.
- Establish the standing review gate (R8) used through production.
- Community advisory input on tone and sensitivities.

## Phase 2 — Vertical slice (Unreal 5)
- One fully-realized mission at target fidelity — recommend **M1 "Al-Amīn"**
  (non-combat, showcases visuals + the gaze/choice/narration systems and the
  bodiless POV).
- Build the reusable core systems from `08-technical-architecture.md`
  (Prophet-POV controller, narration/sourced-dialogue, gaze interaction,
  reverence FX).
- Validate the "realistic, CS-quality" art target in practice.

## Phase 3 — Act I + the restraint mechanic
- Complete Act I and prototype the signature **restraint/mercy** mechanic
  (toward Ṭāʾif and, later, Fatḥ Makka).
- First external playtests measuring *empathy and reflection*, not engagement.

## Phase 4 — Full production
- Remaining acts, the three era-accurate battle missions, full localization
  (Arabic-first, RTL), accessibility, and audio.
- Continuous scholarly review through the gate.

## Phase 5 — Release & stewardship
- Content guidance, comfort/skip options, and an educational codex finalized.
- Launch PC-first; post-launch support and a "behind the history" companion
  mode citing sources.

## Open questions to resolve with stakeholders
- **Scope of playable combat** — keep to the three historical battles only?
  (Current design: yes.)
- **Audio philosophy** — instrumentation vs. voice/percussion-only option, to
  respect differing views.
- **First-person-as-Prophet vs. companion-only** — current design uses *both*;
  scholarly review in Phase 1 may refine the balance.
- **Target rating/audience** — current guardrails aim for a broad, including
  younger, audience (no gore, no graphic torture).
- **Funding & stewardship** — studio, grant, or institutional backing; who
  holds the scholarly-review authority long-term.
