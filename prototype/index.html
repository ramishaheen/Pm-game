A first-person journey through the life of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

Prototype vignette — “Al-Amīn” (The Trustworthy)

Mecca, c. 605 CE. A flood has damaged the Kaʿba. As the clans rebuild it, they fall into a bitter dispute over who will have the honor of setting the sacred Black Stone in place — and they are close to bloodshed. They have agreed to accept the judgment of the next person to arrive. That person is you.

This demo shows the game’s non-combat, conscience-driven core: you resolve a conflict with wisdom, not force. You are present in first person, with no avatar and no depiction — you experience the world through his eyes. His words appear as on-screen narration drawn from historical tradition.

Content note: a respectful historical experience. Press Esc at any time to pause.
